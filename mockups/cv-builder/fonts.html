Fonts for the CV

The same slice of your CV in each font, at real print size (body 10.5 pt), with the new name spacing (0.22 em on the first name, 0.18 em on the bold surname). All of them are free fonts that can go into the PDF.

How to read this: serifs (the first five) look more classic and "printed"; sans-serifs (the last four) look more modern and technical. Widths differ a lot: a narrower font (EB Garamond, Source Sans 3) fits more on a page; a wider one (Lora, Inter, IBM Plex) needs more room. Whichever you pick, I'd put it in the PDF itself, so the preview and the PDF use the same file.
